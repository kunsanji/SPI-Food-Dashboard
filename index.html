    Reading a report

    Violations 1 to 29 on the inspection form are risk factors. These are the problems most likely to make someone sick, like food kept at the wrong temperature or an employee not washing their hands. Violations 30 to 58 are upkeep: cleaning, repairs and staff training.

    We add a label to each inspection so the list is easier to scan:

    Needs attention 3 or more risk factor violations, or a repeat violation from an earlier inspection
    Minor issues any other violations
    No violations found
    Older report form inspections before 2019, which used a different form (why)

    Sangamon County doesn't give letter grades or pass/fail results. An inspection shows what the inspector saw that day, and many problems are fixed on the spot. More about the data
