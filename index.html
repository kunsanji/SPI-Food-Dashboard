    How to read this

    Risk factor findings (items 1 to 29 on the form) are the kinds of problems that can directly make people sick, like food kept at the wrong temperature. Upkeep findings (items 30 to 58) are about cleaning, repairs, good practice and required staff training.

    Labels: Needs attention means 3 or more risk factor findings, or a problem repeated from last time. Minor issues means any other finding. No issues written up means the inspector recorded nothing. Older report form marks reports from before 2019, when the county used a different form (see why).

    There is no pass or fail grade in Sangamon County. Each visit is a snapshot of one day. More about the data.
