Reading these charts

  • A violation is something an inspector saw and wrote down. Changes over time can come from changes in inspection practice, staffing or the form itself, not only from changes in how food is handled.
  • Points based on fewer than 10 routine inspections are left out because they swing too much to mean anything.
  • The most recent month is only partly finished.
