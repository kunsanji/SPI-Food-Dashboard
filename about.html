About the data

Where the data comes from

The Sangamon County Department of Public Health (SCDPH) inspects restaurants, grocery stores, schools, day cares and other places that serve food. It posts every inspection on its inspection website, which is the official record. This site copies those results and adds explanations. We don't change, add or remove any violations.

How often it updates

  • Every night, a program checks the county website and copies any new inspections. It also re-checks the last 14 days in case a report was entered late.
  • You can download the data: inspections (CSV), violations (CSV), update log (JSON).
  • Refresh now reloads the latest copy in your browser.
  • If the data is more than a day old, you'll see Run update now. It opens the update on GitHub, where the people who run this site can start it. The page will refresh on its own when it's done.

What the labels mean

Sangamon County doesn't use labels or grades. We added these to make the list easier to scan.

Needs attention 3 or more risk factor violations, or any repeat violation.

Minor issues Some violations, but fewer than 3 risk factor violations and no repeats.

No violations found The inspector didn't write up any violations.

Older report form Before 2019, the county used a different inspection form with different numbers, and it didn't separate risk factors from upkeep. We show those violations the way the inspector wrote them, without a label or an explanation.

We also shorten the inspection types. The county's "Original" is shown as Routine, and "1st Re-Inspect" through "6th Re-Inspect" are shown as Follow-up.

Things to keep in mind

  • There are no grades, scores or rankings, from the county or from us.
  • An inspection shows what the inspector saw that day. Many problems are fixed while the inspector is there or soon after.
  • Places with more complicated food get inspected more often, so don't compare the number of violations between, say, a restaurant and a gas station.
  • The explanation for each violation is general. We wrote one for each line on the inspection form. The inspector's notes under it say what was actually seen.
  • Inspector names are on the county website, but we don't show them here.
  • If anything here doesn't match the county website, the county website is right. Please let us know.
