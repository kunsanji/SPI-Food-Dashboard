About the data

Where these reports come from, how often they update, and what they can and can't tell you.

Where it comes from

The Sangamon County Department of Public Health (SCDPH) inspects restaurants, stores, schools, day cares and other food businesses in the county. It publishes every inspection on its public inspection portal, run by Custom Data Processing. That portal is the official record. This site copies the same records and adds plain-language explanations; it does not change or add findings.

How it stays up to date

  • Every night a small program visits the county portal, one page at a time, and copies inspections from the last 14 days (to catch reports entered late) plus anything new.
  • It saves them as data files that this site reads. You can download them: inspections (CSV), findings (CSV), update log (JSON).
  • Refresh now reloads those files in your browser. It is free and does not contact the county.
  • If the last update is more than a day old, Run update now opens the update job on GitHub, where the site's maintainers can start it by hand. The page then checks every 30 seconds and reloads when new data arrives.

What the labels mean

Needs attention 3 or more risk factor findings, or any repeat violation, on that visit.

Minor issues At least one finding, but fewer than 3 risk factor findings and no repeats.

No issues written up The inspector recorded no findings.

These labels were made for this site to help people scan the list. They are not county ratings. Visit types are shortened too: "Original" on the county report is shown as Routine, and "1st Re-Inspect" through "6th Re-Inspect" as Follow-up.

Limits

  • No scores. Sangamon County does not grade or pass/fail food businesses, and neither does this site. There are no rankings here.
  • One visit is a snapshot. It shows what one inspector saw on one day. Many problems are corrected during the visit or soon after.
  • Counts are not comparable across types of place. Places with more complex food handling are inspected more often and have more items that can be checked.
  • The plain-language lines are general. They explain what each form item covers, written by hand for this site. The inspector's own note, shown under each finding, says what was actually seen.
  • Inspector names appear on the county portal but are not shown here.
  • Errors. If something here does not match the county portal, the portal is right. Please tell us.
