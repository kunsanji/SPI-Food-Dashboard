    How a report is built

    • Items 1 to 29: risk factors and public health interventions
    • Items 30 to 56: good retail practices (upkeep)
    • Items 57 and 58: Illinois training rules
    • Each finding cites a 2022 FDA Food Code rule
    • No grades or pass/fail, and each inspection is one visit

    What the data can't tell you

    • What happened on the other 364 days
    • Whether a problem was fixed on the spot
    • Illness that was never reported
    • How places compare when they are inspected at different rates

    The law in Illinois

    • Food handlers: approved training within 30 days of hire
    • Restaurant food handlers: retrain every 3 years
    • At least one approved option for $15 or less
    • A certified food protection manager in charge

    410 ILCS 625/3.05 and 3.06. IDPH Food Handler FAQ.
